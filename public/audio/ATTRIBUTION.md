# Audio sources

All three recordings below come from **AUDIO M4 WAV** by [Andreabarata](https://freesound.org/people/Andreabarata/sounds/335345/), released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The creator describes it as a BMW M4 engine starting, with revving at the end. The recording was published in 2016; its exact model year and generation are not specified.

- `m4-start.mp3`: the ignition and initial running sound, trimmed from approximately 3.55–8.15 seconds of the source, with a high-pass filter and short fades.
- `m4-rev.mp3`: a rev excerpt from approximately 180.7–187.0 seconds, with the same filtering and short fades.
- `m4-idle.mp3`: a trimmed and looped idle derivative from [bnbong/aMBiW](https://github.com/bnbong/aMBiW/blob/main/public/assets/audio/audio-attribution.txt).

The start and rev excerpts were made from the MP3 copy of [the same recording on Pixabay](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/city-audio-m4-wav-50124/), retrieved through [this GitHub mirror](https://github.com/davit70/GOA-Homework/blob/HEAD/level%2019/classwork/freesound_community-audio-m4-wav-50124.mp3). That copy matches the original's duration and closely matches an independently credited M4 excerpt from the same source. The excerpts were re-encoded for the web; no synthetic engine sounds were added.
